About Big Red

Engadine's Local Spot, Takeaway or Dine-In

We've been pouring coffee and making brekkie on Station Street since 2016! Our goal hasn't changed: great coffee, honest food and a space to call home.

We cook everything to order, we bake every morning and brew your coffee just how you like it! We love our community and hope that you can join us.
