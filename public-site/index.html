About Big Red

A local spot to slow down before the platform

We've been pouring coffee and plating brekkie on Station Street for years, and the mission hasn't changed: great coffee, honest food, and a table that feels like yours the second you sit down.

From the first flat white of the morning to the last thickshake before pick-up, we cook everything to order — sourdough toasted properly, eggs the way you actually asked for them, and a burger patty that's never seen a freezer.
